Hardware Troubleshooting Guide

1
Check ESP32 Power & Wi-Fi:

Ensure ESP32 LED is solid. Verify 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi credentials in your Arduino/ESP-IDF sketch.

2
PZEM-004T Serial RX/TX:

Verify PZEM TX connects to ESP32 RX (e.g. GPIO16) and PZEM RX connects to ESP32 TX (GPIO17).

3
ThingSpeak Channel Rate Limits:

Free ThingSpeak accounts require minimum 15-second interval between uploads.
